Key observations

Generated by fixed rules from the tables below (no language model). Each carries its denominator.

  • Coverage first: a mechanism could be assigned for 32 of 105 active drug-type trials (30%). The other 73 (70%) have no classified mechanism, so statements below describe the classified subset only, not the whole field.
  • Most frequent mechanisms within the classified subset (unique active trials; a trial can count in more than one): Neuroinflammation & immune (7 trials; 5 distinct curated assets or compounds), Neurotrophic & cell-based (4 trials; 2 distinct curated assets or compounds), Other gene-directed (4 trials; 1 distinct curated asset or compound). Trial counts are not programme counts: several individualised (n-of-1) or extension studies can share one asset.
  • Gene- or target-directed buckets (SOD1, C9orf72, FUS, ATXN2, TDP-43 biology, other gene-directed) cover 13 of the 32 classified active trials (41%). Across all 105 active drug-type trials the share is only bounded: between 12% and 82%, depending on what the unclassified trials turn out to be.
  • Modality is explicit (curated asset or keyword) for 52 of 140 active test-intervention records; 86 rely on the registry intervention type (e.g. DRUG presumed small molecule) and 2 are unresolved.
  • Target mapping is implemented for 6 of 30 Open Targets genes listed; 24 are not assessed and carry no flag. Every mapped gene has at least one matching trial (any status) in the snapshot. Mapped genes whose matching trials are all inactive in the snapshot: ATXN2, C9orf72.
  • Registered primary-completion milestones (active, highest phase ≥ 2) by year: 2026: 2, 2027: 30, 2028: 12. These dates mark the end of primary-outcome data collection as registered, not result announcements.
  • 24 active drug-type trials (23%) list at least one site in Asia; 14 list no sites at all.

d · Method

Interventions are first given a role from the registry's arm structure (experimental, background, comparator, control-arm, placebo/sham, non-therapeutic, or unresolved). Only test-role drug-type interventions (experimental, experimental-inferred, unresolved-role) drive trial labels; background, comparator and control-arm treatments are kept in separate columns. Each test intervention is then labelled by: manual override → curated asset table (data/curation/asset_curation.csv: 37 rules, each with its source status) → name keywords (19 rules) → description keywords (modality only; mechanism as candidate) → registry-type fallback (modality only). The tier and rule behind every label are in data/interventions.csv.

ClinicalTrials.gov coverage is not a complete global or preclinical landscape. Registry activity does not establish clinical efficacy. This prototype has not been scientifically validated.
